## Custom motion created in code

The globe linework, system-node overlays, connected-system cards, capability UI, data bars, cyber-resilience rings, delivery roadmap, scan lines and system telemetry are generated with HTML/CSS/JavaScript. The non-continent wireframe globe remains in the Homepage What We Do experience for systems integration.

The revised Capabilities page also uses local CSS-built diagrams for identity and verification, institutional data exchange, registries and records, and border/trade systems. These are decorative illustrations and do not claim to depict live PLG Solutions systems.

## Production note
These visuals are illustrative only. They should not be presented as PLG Solutions client sites, PLG Solutions-owned facilities, or completed PLG Solutions projects unless that is factually true. Replace temporary visuals later with approved project photography or commissioned brand visuals.

## Prototype stats / partner strip

- The homepage statistics are illustrative placeholder figures only and are labelled as such in the interface. Replace them with verified PLG Solutions metrics before public launch.
- The moving partner marks are fictional CSS-built placeholder brands. They intentionally avoid using real-company trademarks or implying actual PLG Solutions partnerships while the layout is being prototyped.
